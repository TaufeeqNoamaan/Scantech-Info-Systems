## Brand & Style

This design system reflects three decades of mission-critical enterprise delivery. It rejects tech-startup trends—such as gratuitous glassmorphic blurs, hyper-saturated neon accents, and claustrophobic dark terminals—in favor of a grounded, reassuring, and highly legible visual language. The design aligns with executive B2B sensibilities: calm, institutional, steady, and clear.

### Key Principles
- **Institutional Weight:** Interfaces project stability through clear grid systems, solid geometry, and confident typography rather than decorative flourishes.
- **Editorial Legibility:** Layouts read like a high-level briefing document or trusted institutional report, prioritizing effortless scannability and generous vertical space.
- **Quiet Precision:** High-contrast text pairs with muted slate boundaries, ensuring users can parse complex systems, governance frameworks, and service catalogs without cognitive fatigue.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in established enterprise authority, utilizing an executive deep navy for anchors and typography, balanced with warm-toned off-white backgrounds and restrained accents.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#0F2942`):** Carries structural authority. Used for high-emphasis headlines, active navigation states, primary button backgrounds, and strong boundaries.
- **Secondary Slate Spruce (`#0F766E`):** Provides a mature, grounded accent for key indicators, metrics, verification badges, and secondary callouts.
- **Tertiary Executive Blue (`#1D4ED8`):** Reserved strictly for actionable elements, functional interactive states, and inline links.
- **Neutral Slate (`#64748B`):** Governs secondary metadata, supporting captions, inactive tabs, and subtle layout divisions.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` provides a warm, low-glare foundation across large viewport canvases.
- **Card/Container Surface:** `#FFFFFF` elevates individual components with crisp contrast.
- **Muted Container:** `#F1F5F9` houses secondary groupings, table headers, and read-only informational modules.
- **Structural Outlines:** `#E2E8F0` delivers clear structural definition without visual clutter.

## Typography

The type system uses Plus Jakarta Sans across all roles to present modern clarity anchored by geometric balance.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Set with tighter letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) and solid weights (`600` or `700`) in primary navy (`#0F2942`) to create immediate executive presence.
- **Body Text:** Uses generous line heights (e.g., `26px` on `16px` body) to guarantee sustained readability during reviews of enterprise statements of work, SLA documents, and technical roadmaps.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Retain a medium-to-semibold weight (`600`) with slight letter spacing to ensure crisp rendering at smaller sizes without relying on heavy uppercase treatments.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a 12-column fixed-max-width grid with generous breathing room to avoid information density overload.

### Structural Grid
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 1280px max-width container, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 40px (`2.5rem`) outer canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) margins. Content shifts from four-column enterprise feature grids to two-column arrangements.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Grid structures stack vertically.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Use `space-xl` (40px) to separate major content clusters and define sections within viewports.
- Restrict `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) strictly to internal element alignments, such as icon-to-text gaps and compact metadata groups.
- Avoid compressed tables; enforce ample vertical row spacing to ensure effortless data scanning.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through high-contrast boundary definition and flat tonal surfaces rather than heavy dropped shadows.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFC` base layer. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Depth is reinforced with an ambient, diffused shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Popovers):** Used when raising an actionable container or revealing contextual menus. Outlines darken to `#CBD5E1` while the ambient shadow expands smoothly: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Executive Drawers):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`, combined with a warm slate backdrop overlay: `rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design uses conservative, structured geometry to project reliability and engineering rigor.

### Rounding Standard
- **Inputs, Buttons, and Controls:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius. Maintains a tailored, architectural silhouette.
- **Cards and Modals (`rounded-lg`):** `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius. Provides clean separation while fitting enterprise interfaces.
- **Segmented Badges & Metrics Containers (`rounded-xl`):** `12px` (`0.75rem`) max. Retains structure without softening into playful curves.
- Fully circular or pill-shaped buttons are prohibited to prevent a casual consumer aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0F2942` fill, text in `#FFFFFF`, with a 4px corner radius. Padding: `10px 20px`. Hover state darkens gently to `#091A2B`.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF` with a `1px solid #CBD5E1` border and `#0F2942` text. Hover shifts background to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Transparent background with `#1D4ED8` text, standard underline appearing only on hover.

### Input Fields
- Built on `#FFFFFF` backgrounds using a `1px solid #CBD5E1` boundary and 4px radius.
- Padding: `10px 14px`.
- Active focus state employs a clean `1px solid #1D4ED8` ring with a `2px` faint focus halo: `0 0 0 3px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.12)`. No high-saturation glows.

### Cards & Enterprise Panels
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` fills and a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- Headers within enterprise cards feature an optional baseline border separating title actions from core content modules.
- Minimum internal padding: `24px` (`1.5rem`).

### Chips & Status Indicators
- **Operational / Healthy:** `#F0FDF4` fill, `#15803D` text, `#BBF7D0` subtle border.
- **Managed / In Progress:** `#F0FDFA` fill, `#0F766E` text, `#CCFBF1` border.
- **Informational / Milestone:** `#EFF6FF` fill, `#1D4ED8` text, `#DBEAFE` border.
- Always rendered with a 4px radius, medium weight typography, and zero gloss or sheen.

### Enterprise Metrics Displays
- Key performance and SLA statistics place large headline values (`32px` or `48px`) in `#0F2942` over a small, uppercase muted label (`#64748B`, `12px`, letter spacing `0.05em`).
- Container panels for metrics sit on a `#F8FAFC` background with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
